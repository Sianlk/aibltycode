Delete your AIblty account and data

AIblty lets account holders permanently delete their account and associated learning data.

Delete inside the app

  1. Sign in to AIblty.
  2. Open Settings → Privacy.
  3. Select Delete my account.
  4. Type DELETE when asked to confirm.

This cancels active AIblty subscriptions and deletes the account, profile and associated learning records.

Request deletion without the app

If you cannot sign in or have already uninstalled the app, email the privacy contact below from the email address associated with your AIblty account. Include the words “AIblty account deletion request”.

Request account deletion

What is deleted

Your authentication account, profile, learning progress, badges/owned learning items, skill/challenge progress and user-linked battle participation are deleted or anonymised. Active subscriptions are cancelled.

What may be retained

Payment providers may retain transaction records where required for tax, accounting, fraud prevention, chargebacks or other legal obligations. Those records are not kept as an active AIblty learning account.

See the AIblty Privacy Policy for more detail.
