AIblty Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 September 2026. Applies to the website at aiblty.com and the AIblty Android app (com.aibltycode.app).

1. Who we are

AIblty is an online learning platform that teaches coding, AI, data, IT and digital skills from absolute beginner to professional level. The data controller is the operator of AIblty. Privacy enquiries: privacy@aiblty.com. We reply within 30 days.

We do not collect precise location, contacts, photos, microphone audio, health data, SMS or your device's advertising ID. We do not show adverts. We never sell personal data.

3. Children

AIblty offers a Kids Mode with simplified language. Accounts for under-13s (or the equivalent age in your country) must be created and managed by a parent or guardian, who consents to this policy on the child's behalf. Kids Mode does not contain adverts, external links to social platforms, or open user-to-user messaging. Parental controls are protected by a PIN. A parent may request access to, or deletion of, a child's data at any time by emailing us.

4. AI features

When you use the AI tutor or the AI Builder Studio, the text you send is passed to an AI model provider purely to generate your answer. We do not send your email address or account identifiers with it, and the content is not used to train public models. Please do not type personal or confidential information into AI chat boxes.

5. Legal bases (UK/EU GDPR)

  • Contract — account, progress saving, subscription access.
  • Consent — optional analytics, marketing emails, non-essential cookies. You can withdraw consent at any time in Settings.
  • Legitimate interests — security, abuse prevention, fixing crashes.
  • Legal obligation — tax and accounting records.

6. Who processes data for us

Our hosting and database provider (account data and progress, encrypted in transit and at rest), our AI model provider (AI chat requests only), and our payment provider (subscription payments; card details are handled entirely by them). Each acts under contract and may not use your data for their own purposes.

7. Security

All traffic uses HTTPS/TLS. Passwords are stored only as salted hashes. Database access is restricted by per-user row-level security rules, so one learner can never read another learner's data. Scoring, rewards and quiz answers are validated on the server.

8. Retention

Account and progress data is kept while your account is open. If you delete your account, personal data is erased within 30 days. Anonymous, non-identifying learning statistics may be retained. Billing records are kept for 7 years where the law requires it.

9. Your rights

You can access, correct, export or delete your data, object to processing, or withdraw consent. In the app: Settings → Privacy lets you turn analytics off, erase locally stored analytics, and request account deletion. Or email privacy@aiblty.com. You may also complain to your data protection authority (in the UK, the ICO).

10. Account and data deletion

To delete your AIblty account and all associated personal data, open Settings → Privacy → Delete my account in the app, or email privacy@aiblty.com from your registered address. Deletion is completed within 30 days; subscription billing records are retained only as long as tax law requires.

11. International transfers

Our providers may process data outside the UK/EEA. Where they do, transfers rely on UK International Data Transfer Agreements or EU Standard Contractual Clauses.

12. Cookies and local storage

We use essential storage to keep you signed in and to hold your progress when you are offline. Optional analytics storage is used only with your consent and holds no name, email or answer text.

13. Changes

We will update this page and change the date above when this policy changes. Material changes are announced in the app.

14. Contact

Privacy enquiries and data requests: privacy@aiblty.com.
