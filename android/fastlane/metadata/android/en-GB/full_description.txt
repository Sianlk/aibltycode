HOW AIblty TEACHES

Every subject follows a mastery loop designed to make knowledge retrievable rather than merely familiar:

1. Five-year-old-simple explanation — technical terms are introduced in plain language with no assumed background.
2. Memory hooks — the MIND method links Meaning, Image, Name and Doing.
3. Guided practice — learners type, configure, query, diagram, analyse or build a real artefact.
4. No-peeking retrieval — learners must pull the idea back from memory.
5. Teach-back — explain the idea simply enough that a young beginner could understand it.
6. Fast recall — repeat until the key idea can be retrieved accurately in under 60 seconds.
7. Professional transfer — explain where, how and why the skill is used in real work.
8. Spaced review — successful recall schedules the next memory check automatically.
